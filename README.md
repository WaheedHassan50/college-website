# Government Graduate College Hafizabad — Official Web Portal
**حکومتی گریجویٹ کالج حافظ آباد**

A modern, responsive, and feature-rich educational web portal built for **Government Graduate College Hafizabad (Punjab, Pakistan)**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Functionalities

1. **Official Identity & Leadership**:
   - Official Vector Crest (`assets/images/logo.svg`) with Quranic motto (*رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا*), Punjab Government green and gold theme.
   - Comprehensive **Message from the Principal (Prof. Muhammad Inayat Bhatti)** with portrait graphics and educational vision.
   - History of the college spanning 60+ years since establishment in 1958.

2. **Faculty Directory (`faculty.html`)**:
   - Complete directory of over 65 faculty members across Science, Mathematics, Computer Science, English, Urdu, and Humanities.
   - Real-time search by name, qualification, or research specialization.
   - Department filter tabs and interactive professor profile modals with consultation hours.

3. **Academic Programs (`academics.html`)**:
   - 14+ BS (4-Year) Honors Degrees affiliated with the **University of the Punjab (PU)**:
     - BS Computer Science (BS CS)
     - BS Mathematics
     - BS English (Literature & Linguistics)
     - BS Physics & Chemistry
     - BS Botany & Zoology
     - BS Urdu & Economics
   - Intermediate Programs affiliated with **BISE Gujranwala** (F.Sc Pre-Medical, F.Sc Pre-Engineering, ICS, I.Com, FA).
   - Associate Degree Programs (ADS & ADA).
   - PU Semester System grading framework (GPA / CGPA scale & 75% attendance policy).

4. **Online Admissions 2026 Engine (`admissions.html`)**:
   - **Interactive 4-Step Online Admission Wizard**:
     - Step 1: Personal Details & Contact
     - Step 2: Matric & Intermediate Marks
     - Step 3: Program Preferences
     - Step 4: Instant Printable Official Application Slip with Application ID!
   - **Aggregate Merit Calculator**: Calculates Punjab University standard merit formula (30% Matric + 70% Intermediate + 20 Marks Hafiz-e-Quran) with eligible program recommendations.
   - **Official 3-Copy Bank Fee Challan Generator**: Generates Bank Copy, College Copy, and Student Copy for National Bank of Pakistan (NBP) Hafizabad Branch with one-click print feature.

5. **Campus Facilities & Infrastructure (`facilities.html`)**:
   - Allama Iqbal Central Library (25,000+ books & HEC Digital Library access).
   - High-Tech AI & Computer Laboratories (150+ Core i7 PCs with 24/7 solar backup).
   - Advanced Physics, Chemistry, Zoology, and Botany wet laboratories.
   - Sports Stadium (Cricket ground, Football, Badminton, Table Tennis).
   - Quaid-e-Azam Auditorium and Student Transport fleet.

6. **Official Notice Board (`notices.html`)**:
   - Filter notices by Admissions, Merit Lists, Examinations, and Scholarships (PEEF/Ehsaas).
   - Live Notice ticker / marquee on top of the home page.
   - Interactive notification reader modal with printable circular downloads.

7. **Student & Faculty Services Portal (`student-portal.html`)**:
   - Online Examination Roll Number Slip Generator & Print layout.
   - Online Semester Result Card / Transcript inquiry with GPA calculator.
   - Downloadable class lecture timetables.
   - Staff LMS portal login mockup.

8. **Campus Gallery (`gallery.html`)**:
   - Filterable photo gallery (Events, Sports Gala, Labs, Campus Life) with Lightbox popup.

9. **Contact & Location (`contact.html`)**:
   - Official phone numbers, emails, office hours, and Google Maps location guide.
   - Interactive inquiry submission form with toast notifications.

10. **Modern UI/UX**:
    - Dark Mode & Light Mode switcher with local storage memory.
    - Quick Universal Search Modal with keyboard shortcut (`Ctrl + K`).
    - Fully responsive across smartphones, tablets, laptops, and 4K screens.

---

## 🚀 How to Run the Website

Double-click `Open_Website.bat` or open `index.html` directly in any web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Safari). No web server or node installation is needed!
