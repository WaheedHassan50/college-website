Official Communiqué

Principal's Address to Students & Parents

It is a matter of profound privilege and honor for me to lead Government Graduate College Hafizabad, an institution that has nurtured generations of scholars, civil servants, doctors, engineers, military officers, and patriotic citizens since 1958.

Our pedagogical philosophy is grounded in three pillars: Rigorous Scholarship, Unwavering Ethical Integrity, and Active Co-curricular Engagement. We do not just teach textbooks; we instill curiosity, analytical thinking, and civic duty into our students.

With the transition to the 4-Year BS Honors program under the auspices of the University of the Punjab, we have upgraded our IT labs with high-speed fiber connectivity, enriched our central library with international journals, and equipped our physical science labs with precision instrumentation.

To our dear students: You are the architects of Pakistan's future. Utilize the resources of this college with diligence, respect your teachers, and always strive to leave a legacy of excellence.

Prof. Muhammad Inayat Bhatti

Our Vision

To be recognized as a premier center of higher academic learning in Punjab, fostering transformative education, scientific innovation, critical enquiry, and human values that empower students to lead meaningful and constructive lives in a rapidly evolving global society.

Our Mission

To provide equitable, affordable, and high-standard higher education across Science, Arts, Humanities, and Information Technology; to cultivate ethical values, intellectual integrity, and social responsibility; and to groom youth as capable contributors to national development.

Historical Evolution

Six Decades of Academic Heritage

1958

Inception of Govt. Degree College Hafizabad

Established as an intermediate and bachelor-level college to serve the educational aspirations of the youth of Hafizabad and surrounding rural tehsils.

1995

Expansion of Science & Commerce Wings

New dedicated laboratory blocks for Physics, Chemistry, and Zoology were inaugurated alongside full accreditation for I.Com and B.Com degree streams.

2010

Launch of BS (4-Year) Honors Degrees

Upgraded to a Post-Graduate / Graduate college with the launch of flagship 4-year undergraduate BS programs affiliated with the University of the Punjab.

2026

Digital Smart Campus & 14+ BS Disciplines

Today, Govt. Graduate College Hafizabad boasts over 3,800 students, modern AI computing labs, solar-backed energy, and top positions in BISE Gujranwala.
