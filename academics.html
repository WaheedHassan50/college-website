PU Grading Framework

Semester Examination & Grading Rules

Our BS 4-Year degrees follow the rigorous Semester System mandated by the University of the Punjab and the Higher Education Commission (HEC). Each academic year is divided into Fall and Spring Semesters of 18 weeks each.

Continuous Assessment (100 Marks per course):
Mid-Term Exam (35%), Final-Term Exam (40%), Sessional/Assignments/Quizzes (25%).
Minimum Passing Criteria:
A candidate must secure at least 50% marks in each theory & practical paper, and maintain a minimum CGPA of 2.00 to qualify for degree award.
Mandatory 75% Attendance:
Students failing to maintain 75% lecture and practical attendance are barred from taking university terminal exams.
