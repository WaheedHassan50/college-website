Message from the Principal

Welcome to Government Graduate College Hafizabad, the historic beacon of higher education and moral training in our district. For over six decades, this venerable institution has served society by imparting knowledge that builds character, instills critical thinking, and prepares confident young minds for global challenges.

Education is not merely the accumulation of facts; it is the ignition of truth, honor, and patriotic dedication. At GGC Hafizabad, we take immense pride in our highly qualified and research-oriented faculty, modern computerized laboratories, extensive central library, and vibrant sports culture. We believe in providing an inclusive, academically rigorous, and spiritually uplifting environment.

I warmly welcome all prospective students seeking admission in our Intermediate and BS (4-Year) degree programs. May your time here be fruitful, transformative, and inspiring!
