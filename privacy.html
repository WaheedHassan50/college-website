Privacy & Data Policy

Government Graduate College Hafizabad is committed to protecting the privacy of prospective applicants, enrolled students, faculty members, and visitors who use our online web portal.

1. Information Collection

Personal information provided during online admission inquiries (such as student name, CNIC, academic marks, and contact telephone) is collected solely for institutional admission and student registration records.

2. Use of Information

Information submitted is reviewed by the College Admission Committee and affiliated university/board examination bodies (University of the Punjab & BISE Gujranwala). Information is never sold or shared with commercial entities.

3. Official Contact

For inquiries regarding student records or data correction, please contact the Principal's Office at info@ggchafizabad.edu.pk or call 0547-521400.
